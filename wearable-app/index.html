This page sends a heartbeat to the wearable roughly once a second while connected — that's what tells the firmware a phone is present, so it stops treating the link as lost. Distances update at up to 15 Hz (about every 65 ms); ~80 cm is the danger threshold, ~150 cm caution, ~250 cm aware. Say "find <place>", "repeat", "mute", "where am I", or "S O S" any time — the physical button on the wearable does the same five things by press pattern if speech isn't available.
